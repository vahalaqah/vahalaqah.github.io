Privacy Policy

VAHALAQAH Upload · Last updated: 24 November 2026

VAHALAQAH Upload ("the app") is an internal publishing tool operated by VAHALAQAH. It runs only on our own computer and is used only by the VAHALAQAH team to upload and schedule our own videos on our own YouTube, Instagram and Threads accounts. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the basic details of our own YouTube channel (channel ID, name, handle, profile picture) and permission to upload videos to that channel, including titles, descriptions and thumbnails we write.
  • Instagram and Threads (Meta APIs): the basic profile details of our own accounts (account ID, username, profile picture), our daily publishing limit, and permission to publish posts we create.

The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers, followers, commenters or any other person.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show which account is connected, to check that the connection still works, and to publish the videos, captions and schedules that we create. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Storage and security

Access tokens and settings are stored only on our own computer, in a file readable only by our user account. Videos are sent directly from our computer to YouTube, Instagram and Threads. The app has no server, database or analytics of its own.

Retention and deletion

Stored access tokens are kept until we disconnect an account or delete the app's settings, which removes them immediately. You can also revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings, and to Instagram or Threads in each app's Settings > Website permissions.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube through the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram and Threads data is handled under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to VAHALAQAH through our YouTube channel or Instagram.
